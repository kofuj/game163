## The model's best pick of the month got blown out

The Dodgers at 63.8% were the highest single-game confidence on the board since the model's Grade A tier last appeared. San Francisco won 9–3.

That margin — six runs, a blowout — is the kind of result that makes a pick look catastrophically wrong. It wasn't a close game that broke the wrong way. The Giants scored early and kept scoring, and the Dodgers never found a lane back. Roki Sasaki, who the props model projected for 3.9 strikeouts in 4.4 innings, was backed by a lineup that couldn't generate runs against Trevor McDonald.

What the 63.8% probability means: the model estimated the Dodgers would win this game roughly two out of every three times. It also meant they'd lose about one in three. This was that one-in-three game. The model didn't lose its credibility — it lost a game it was expected to lose occasionally.

What the result *doesn't* mean: the Dodgers aren't as good as the model thinks. Their Elo rating and Bayesian win rate are both built on hundreds of games of evidence. One 9–3 loss against a team starting their fourth-best arm doesn't move those priors meaningfully. The model will back the Dodgers again today, at 61.2%.
